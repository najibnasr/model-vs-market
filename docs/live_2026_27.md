# Live season 2026-27: setup and pre-registered expectations

Written on 2026-10-29, **before any 2026-27 result or price was downloaded** into this project.
The models, their parameters and the betting strategy were frozen earlier (steps c to e) and do
not change during the season. Bug fixes, if any, are listed under *Amendments* with their date.

## What runs every week

`make live` re-downloads the current season, takes a timestamped snapshot of
football-data.co.uk's `fixtures.csv` (upcoming matches and prices), replays the frozen models up
to the last match played, predicts every upcoming match and **appends** those predictions to
`reports/live/predictions_log.csv`. The log is committed to git, so each prediction's timestamp
is publicly checkable against the kick-off date.

Season results in the app use walk-forward predictions (the frozen model sees only matches played
before the week being predicted), so they exist for every match, including those played before
the first `make live`. The log adds the stronger guarantee for matches predicted from then on:
the dashboard counts how many results were logged at least a day before kick-off.

## Changes forced by the data, decided before looking

football-data.co.uk stopped publishing Pinnacle prices during 2025-26. Therefore, as planned in
the protocol's fallback rule:

| | Test 2017-25 | Live 2026-27 |
|---|---|---|
| Market reference | Pinnacle close, Shin | Market-average close, Shin |
| Price taken by the strategy | Pinnacle pre-match | Market-average pre-match |
| Optimistic bound | Best price (max) | Best price (max) |
| CLV measured against | Pinnacle close | Market-average close |

The market average failed the fallback check (it is slightly but significantly less sharp than
Pinnacle), so every live comparison is *optimistic for the model*: beating the average is easier
than beating Pinnacle.

## Expectations (checked once, at the end of the season)

1. **Log-loss.** The market-average close beats the model. On the test seasons the gap to the
   Pinnacle close was +0.017; against the slightly weaker average we expect a gap between
   +0.010 and +0.025, with a 95% CI (week-clustered bootstrap) excluding zero.
2. **Duel.** Among matches with a winner (more than one point of probability apart on the
   realised result), the bookmakers win more than half.
3. **CLV.** The main strategy's CLV against the average close is negative, CI excluding zero.
   At the best price it is close to zero or negative.
4. **Controls.** The oracle (betting with closing probabilities) has a clearly positive CLV; the
   base-rate bettor loses about the margin.
5. **Money.** The quarter-Kelly bankroll ends the season below its starting value.

If any of these fails, the failure is reported as it is, with a diagnosis, and no parameter is
changed to "fix" it.

## Holdout 2025-26

2025-26 stays reserved for evaluating the planned v2 (xG features), so the app no longer shows
it. Note: the step-(f) dashboard displayed 2025-26 matches before this rule was applied, and at
least one league-season (Bundesliga 2025-26) was viewed on 2026-10-28. The v2 evaluation must
therefore treat 2025-26 as partly seen and give the live season the main weight.
